Deep Ancestry Package · 8 Weeks

The Missing Weaver of Inverness

Claire Mackenzie's family had spoken of a great-great-grandmother who had "vanished" sometime in the 1860s. A vague family understanding held that she had been "sent away" — the implication, shame. The truth was far more remarkable.

Our researcher Dr. Siobhan O'Brien found Elspeth MacAllister's name on an 1872 passenger manifest aboard the SS Ethiopia. She was 24, listed as "single female, occupation: weaver." She had saved her fare herself over three years working in Inverness mills.

By 1890, according to New York City trade directories, Elspeth operated a small loom workshop employing six women — all recent Scottish and Irish immigrants. A 1905 photograph, found in the Ellis Island archive, shows her outside the workshop, hands on her hips, looking directly at the camera.

"Knowing she chose this — that she wasn't cast out — changed everything I understood about my family." — Claire Mackenzie, 2024
