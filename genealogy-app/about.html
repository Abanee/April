Our Story

Seventeen Years of
Rediscovering Families

Rootbound was built on a single belief: that every family's history is worth knowing, and that the tools to find it should be available to everyone — not just the privileged.

Our Mission

Every Person Deserves to Know
Where They Came From

Genealogy has long been associated with privilege — those with time, money, and the right cultural background to find records. We've spent 17 years dismantling that barrier.

Our team includes specialists in complex, under-indexed archives: African diasporic lineages, post-colonial records, Eastern European Jewish communities, and more. We don't just research the easy cases.

The end product is never just a document — it's a narrative. A story your family can hold, read, and pass down.

Our History

From a Notebook to a
Global Research Firm

2009

Founded in Edinburgh

Dr. Siobhan O'Brien begins Rootbound as a one-woman practice, offering paper-based genealogy reports to clients in Scotland and Ireland. The first client is traced to 14th-century County Clare.

2012

First International Team Member

Prof. Klaus Bergmann joins from Berlin, opening access to Central European archives and enabling German, Austrian, and Polish research for the first time.

2015

Digital Dashboard Launch

Rootbound launches its client portal, giving families real-time progress updates, document uploads, and a living family tree that grows as research progresses.

2018

DNA Integration Programme

Dr. Amara Diallo joins as Head of Genetic Genealogy, pioneering a programme that integrates commercial DNA testing results with archival research — particularly for clients with West African or diasporic heritage.

2021

2,000th Family Milestone

Rootbound serves its 2,000th client family, expanding to a team of 12 specialists covering 14 countries. The firm is awarded the Association of Professional Genealogists' Excellence in Research Award.

2026

Present Day

Over 4,800 families served. Partnerships with national archives in seven countries. An expanded team of 18 genealogists, historians, and genetic specialists — and the same commitment to honest, rigorous, humanising research.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Rigorous Accuracy

Every fact we include is verified against at least two independent primary sources. We prefer to note a gap in the record honestly rather than fill it with speculation.

Human Sensitivity

Family history can uncover difficult truths — adoption, illegitimacy, tragedy, violence. We approach every finding with care, contextualising rather than sensationalising.

Absolute Privacy

Your family's data is yours. End-to-end encrypted, GDPR-compliant, never sold or shared. Living individuals in your tree remain private by default — always.
