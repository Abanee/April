— Our Story

Paint First.
Ask Later.

CRUX started as an illegal operation. Two friends, Mateo and Lena, spent 2018 painting abandoned industrial buildings around Barcelona's Zona Franca. They weren't hired — they just believed the walls needed colour.

When a local developer offered to commission them for a new mixed-use complex, the studio was born. That first wall, a 40-metre geometric composition on Carrer de l'Almogàvers, still stands.

Today, CRUX is a collective of 12 full-time artists operating across four continents. We handle everything — concept, permits, scaffolding, painting, and the 5-year maintenance warranty that keeps our work looking sharp long after the cameras leave.

"We didn't build a studio. We built a permission to be loud."
— Mateo Reyes, Co-founder

— What We Stand For

The Code.

01

Art belongs on the street.

Galleries are for Sundays. We believe the greatest art canvases in the world are the facades of buildings that millions of people walk past daily — often without looking up.

02

The artist's vision is the brief.

We guide clients on style, scale, and concept — but we don't compromise the artist's language to fit a committee's comfort zone. That's how you get great work.

03

Scale changes everything.

A design that works at A4 rarely works at 40 metres. We think in dimensions. Every concept is stress-tested for the wall before a single nozzle is pressed.

04

Cities remember what they see.

A great mural becomes part of a city's identity. We take that responsibility seriously, which is why every commission comes with our 5-year quality guarantee.
