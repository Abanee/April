Methodology

The Path to Refinement

Every masterpiece begins as a raw vision. Our restoration process respects the tree's natural narrative while applying centuries-old styling techniques.

01

Structural Assessment

Identifying the tree's "front," assessing root health, and visualizing the hidden line of the trunk.

02

Styling & Wiring

Pruning unnecessary growth and using copper or aluminium wire to guide branches into their aesthetic positions.

03

Maturity & Show

Over several seasons, the tree matures in its pot, developing the character and "soul" of a miniature ancient tree.

Patience is the tool.
Note from the Master
