A Real Story from Our Archive

She was born in
Inverness, 1847.
Her family forgot her name.

Elspeth Morag MacAllister emigrated alone at 24 — no husband, no escort — arriving at Ellis Island with a single trunk and a loom. Her five great-grandchildren didn't know she existed until a Rootbound genealogist found her naturalization papers buried in a New York county archive.

"She built something from nothing. Knowing that changed how I see myself." — Claire M., client, 2024
