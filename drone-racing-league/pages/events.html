Environments

CIRCUIT TECH

Every race is built differently. Learn about the environments our pilots must master to win.

URBAN NEON

Master the art of night flight. These circuits feature intense neon gates, multi-story vertical drops, and complex RF interference from city infrastructure. Pilots must rely on pure instinct and low-latency video links.

NATURAL WILD

Technical forest and mountain sprints. Pilots navigate through dense foliage and natural rock formations at 140km/h+. Requires extreme obstacle avoidance skills and reactive throttle control.

STADIUM PRO

The pinnacle of professional FPV. Controlled lighting, massive illuminated gates, and the energy of 20,000+ fans. These tracks are designed for maximum visibility and high-speed overtaking maneuvers.
