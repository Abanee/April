FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

Do I need experience to join the training program? +
Not at all! Our FPV Foundations program starts from absolute zero. We provide simulators, equipment, and patient instructors who will have you flying confidently within your first session.
What equipment do I need to bring? +
Nothing for your first sessions — we provide all drones, goggles, and controllers. Once you're committed to racing, we'll recommend a beginner build kit starting at ~$300.
How do I qualify for professional races? +
After completing the Race Ready program, you'll be eligible to enter DRL Open qualifiers. Top performers earn spots in regional and international events. We have pilots who went from beginner to pro in under 12 months.
Are there locations outside the USA? +
Yes! We have training academies in London, Seoul, Dubai, and Sydney. We're also expanding to 5 new cities in 2025. Remote online coaching is available worldwide through our DRL Virtual Academy.
How can my brand sponsor a DRL event? +
We offer flexible sponsorship packages from title sponsors to pilot kit patches. Send us a message using the form above or email sponsors@drlapex.com. Our partnerships team will get back to you within 48 hours.
