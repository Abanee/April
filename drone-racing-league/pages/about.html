Our Story

ABOUT DRL

Born from a passion for speed and technology — we built the world's most competitive FPV drone racing organization.

Our Mission

PUSHING THE LIMITS OF HUMAN FLIGHT

DRL.APEX was founded in 2017 with a single obsession: make drone racing the world's most exciting sport. We combine cutting-edge technology, elite pilot training, and global competitions into one electrifying ecosystem.

From our first 12-pilot local event to a global league spanning 22 countries and $2M+ in annual prize money, we've never stopped pushing the boundary of what's possible in the air.

Our Journey

LEAGUE TIMELINE

2017
League Founded

Started with 12 pilots at a local warehouse in Austin, TX. First race attracted 300 live spectators.

2018
First International Open

Expanded to 5 countries with the "Global Sky Challenge". Total prize pool crossed $100k for the first time.

2020
FPV Academy Launch

Opened the first official DRL training academy. 500+ students in year one across 3 global locations.

2022
$1M Prize Pool

Broke the 1 million dollar barrier. Season 6 championships watched by 2.4 million online viewers.

2025
Season 8 — Present

240+ active pilots, 22 countries, $2M+ prize pool. The biggest season in DRL.APEX history.

What We Stand For

LEAGUE VALUES

⚡
Speed First

We believe in pushing every limit. Our pilots train at the edge of human reaction time, flying machines that accelerate to 160km/h in under a second.

🎯
Precision Wins

Raw speed is nothing without control. We train pilots to fly with millimeter accuracy through complex gate sequences at full throttle.

🌍
Global Community

From Austin to Seoul, our pilots form a tight-knit global family united by a shared obsession with FPV flight and competitive racing.

💡
Innovation Driven

We don't just race drones; we build the future. Our R&D team works daily to develop the next generation of high-speed propulsion and low-latency video links.
