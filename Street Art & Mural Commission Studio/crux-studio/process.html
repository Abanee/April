Architecture of Intervention

The Lifecycle
of a Mural.

A world-class mural isn't just painted — it's engineered. From the first site survey to the final UV-protective seal, we follow a rigorous technical process.

01

Site Audit & Analysis

We don't just look at the wall; we study it. We analyze the surface material (brick, concrete, stucco), moisture levels, and sun exposure to select the correct primers and pigments for longevity.

02

Conceptual Blueprinting

Our artists transform the brief into architectural-scale blueprints. We use digital mockups to simulate how the design interacts with the building's specific geometry and perspective from street level.

03

Permitting & Legal

Urban art requires navigation. We handle all city permitting, heritage approvals, and insurance requirements, ensuring the project is fully compliant with local regulations.

04

Surface Infrastructure

Safety first. We deploy professional scaffolding, boom lifts, or industrial rope access teams. The surface is deep-cleaned and treated with high-bond industrial primers.

05

Scaling & Layout

We use precision techniques — from the 'doodle grid' system to laser projection — to ensure the digital concept is accurately scaled to the massive physical surface.

06

The Intervention

The painting phase. Using premium low-VOC aerosols and mineral-based facade paints, our artists build the mural layer by layer, starting with large blocks and finishing with fine detail.

— Materials

Built to Outlast.

UV-Resistant Pigments

We use specialized mineral paints from Germany and high-solid aerosols that resist fading even in direct equatorial sun.

Anti-Breathable Sealants

The wall needs to breathe. Our sealants allow moisture to escape while protecting the art from environmental pollutants and rain.

— Preservation

5-Year Warranty.

Urban art is alive. Every commission includes a 5-year maintenance warranty covering peeling, excessive fading, and minor surface vandalism.
