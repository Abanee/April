Everything you need to know about commissioning, pricing, and maintaining world-class urban art.

$01 / Commissioning

A typical mural takes between 4 to 8 weeks from the initial briefing to the final sealing. The actual painting phase usually spans 5 to 14 days, depending on the scale and complexity of the design.

Most of our clients come to us for our unique artistic vision. Our process includes a dedicated concept phase where our artists produce 3 distinct sketches for your feedback. However, we are also happy to work with your existing brand guidelines or specific art direction.

$02 / Pricing & Logistics

Our private interior commissions typically start at $3,500. For large-scale exterior facades, projects generally begin at $12,000. Factors influencing price include wall square footage, surface condition, complexity of the design, and scaffolding requirements.

Yes. CRUX is a global collective. We have painted in over 28 cities worldwide. For international projects, we handle all shipping of materials and artist logistics, though additional travel fees may apply.

$03 / Technical & Maintenance

We can paint on almost any surface including brick, concrete, stucco, metal, and glass. During our site audit, we identify the specific substrate and use the appropriate industrial primers to ensure a permanent bond.

For high-traffic urban areas, we offer a specialized anti-graffiti sacrificial coating. If the mural is tagged, the vandalism can be washed off with a simple high-pressure water treatment without damaging the underlying artwork.
